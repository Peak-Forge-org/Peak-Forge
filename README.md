# Sports Platform

A comprehensive sports platform designed to connect athletes and coaches in one integrated ecosystem for training management, nutrition planning, performance tracking, communication, and sports-related services.

## Overview

The platform aims to simplify the relationship between athletes and coaches by providing tools to manage training programs, monitor progress, communicate directly, and maintain long-term motivation.

It also introduces premium features, virtual coach stores, and reward mechanisms to support athletes, create new opportunities for coaches, and establish a sustainable business model.

## User Roles

### Athlete

Athletes can manage their profiles, access personalized training and nutrition plans, communicate with coaches, record training activities, and monitor their athletic progress.

### Coach

Coaches can manage their profiles and athletes, create and distribute training and nutrition plans, communicate with athletes, organize group discussions, and monitor performance.

## Core Features

### Athlete Features

- Personal profile management
- Access to training and nutrition plans
- Private messaging with coaches
- Coach-managed athlete discussion forums
- Training activity logging
- Basic progress charts
- Training notifications and reminders

### Coach Features

- Coach profile management
- Athlete management
- Training plan creation and distribution
- Nutrition plan creation and distribution
- Private communication with athletes
- Group discussion forums
- Athlete progress monitoring
- Notifications about important athlete activities

### Virtual Coach Store

- Dedicated online storefronts for coaches
- Product listing and management
- Potential store access fees or transaction commissions

## Premium Features

### Athlete Premium

- Advanced progress analytics for weight, body fat, and body measurements
- Personal records and athletic performance tracking
- Monthly progress comparisons
- Automated weekly and monthly reports
- Personalized meal planning
- Calorie and macronutrient tracking
- Food substitution suggestions
- Detailed workout logging, including weights, repetitions, and rest periods
- Monthly PDF performance reports
- Training adherence statistics
- Gamification through XP, levels, badges, streaks, achievements, and leaderboards
- Reward coins, discount codes, and activity-based rewards

### Coach Premium

- Advanced athlete management dashboard
- Athlete categorization and performance overview
- Progress charts across multiple athletes
- Reusable training and nutrition templates
- Program duplication and customization
- Bulk program distribution
- Training calendar
- Automated inactivity reminders
- Notifications for significant changes in athlete performance
- Priority messaging for athletes and coaches

## Business Model

The platform is designed to support multiple revenue streams:

- Premium subscriptions for athletes
- Premium subscriptions for coaches
- Fees or commissions from virtual coach stores
- Partnerships with sports businesses for discounts and rewards

## Product Vision

Our vision is to build an integrated sports ecosystem that:

- Improves communication between athletes and coaches
- Helps athletes stay consistent and motivated
- Provides professional tools for training management and performance analysis
- Enables coaches to manage their athletes more efficiently
- Creates new revenue opportunities for coaches and the platform

## Development Roadmap

Planned development phases:

- [ ] Project setup and technical architecture

- [ ] Training logs and basic progress tracking
- [ ] Notifications and reminders
